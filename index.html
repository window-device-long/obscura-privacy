Privacy
Policy

This Policy explains what information may be processed when you play Obscura, why it is processed, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

Updated: October 5, 2026 · Applies to the Obscura game and related services

Before publication: This is a draft based on the information currently available. The app's advertising, analytics, sign-in, cloud storage, and release configuration have not been provided. Keep only the disclosures that match the shipped app. Conditional examples below do not confirm that Obscura collects that information.

01 / Data scope

Data we may process

The actual categories depend on the features enabled in the version of Obscura you use. The core minesweeper game does not need your real name, contacts, precise location, or microphone. We will not request those permissions unless a specific feature explains why it needs them and asks for permission appropriately.

02 / Use of data

Purposes and legal bases

We process only information needed for the purposes described and under a legal basis appropriate to your location and the feature you use. In Vietnam, consent is an important basis; processing without consent is limited to specific statutory grounds. Where the GDPR applies, possible bases include performance of a contract, legal obligations, consent, or legitimate interests after balancing your rights.

Installing the game alone is not treated as consent to a new purpose. If an optional feature needs data, you may decline and continue using the core game unless that data is genuinely necessary for the feature itself.

03 / Sharing

Recipients and SDKs

Information is provided to processors needed to operate a feature, under appropriate instructions and agreements, or when required by law. Before publication, the developer must review each SDK and advertising arrangement to determine whether it constitutes a “sale” or “sharing” under applicable law. The groups below may be relevant; they are not confirmation that each is integrated:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: app distribution, payment processing, reporting, and platform services; the provider may receive data directly from your device.
  • Analytics and crash reporting: only if the relevant SDK is enabled; identify each provider, data category, purpose, and provider policy before release.
  • Advertising: only if ads are present; disclose the ad network and appropriate consent or opt-out controls by region.
  • Cloud storage or sign-in: only if accounts, sync, or backup are available; identify the provider and data stored.
  • Public authorities: where legally required or necessary to protect safety, rights, or property as permitted by law.

Third-party providers have their own privacy policies. Apple or Google may collect device or store data independently of data received by the developer.

04 / Data lifecycle

Retention and transfers

Data is retained for as long as needed to provide a feature, handle support requests, meet record-keeping obligations, or resolve disputes. When no longer needed, it is deleted, anonymized, or securely destroyed under the applicable process. Progress stored only on your device may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, or change devices. Cloud copies, if any, must be deleted through the relevant feature's process.

Before publication, set actual retention periods for logs, analytics, transactions, and support emails based on the app configuration and provider agreements.

If data is processed or stored outside your country or region, transfers will be made only when applicable legal requirements are met, including any required assessments, safeguards, or notices. Disclose the storage locations and transfer mechanisms used by actual providers.

05 / Your rights

Rights under Vietnamese law

Where Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 applies, you may exercise the rights provided by law, including the right to be informed; consent, refuse, or withdraw consent; access and request correction; request provision, deletion, or restriction of processing; object to processing; complain, denounce, bring legal proceedings, and claim compensation subject to statutory conditions.

Send a request to longnpdevmobile@gmail.com with the subject “Obscura Privacy Request.” Describe the request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant data. We may verify your identity proportionately, request necessary details, and respond within the period required by law. Requests may be limited where permitted by law or where retention is required.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal. Refusing data needed for a feature may prevent that feature from working.

06 / By region

Rights in the EU, EEA, UK, and California

Where the GDPR applies

You may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing; receive data in a portable format; withdraw consent; and complain to a supervisory authority. Some rights depend on the legal basis and statutory exceptions. If we rely on legitimate interests, you may request information about those interests and object where the law allows.

Where California law applies

Eligible consumers may request to know, access, delete, or correct personal information; opt out of “sale” or “sharing” as defined by the CCPA (including certain cross-context behavioral advertising); limit the use of sensitive personal information where applicable; and not be discriminated against for exercising their rights. Obscura will provide an appropriate request or opt-out mechanism if the app's activities fall within the law's scope.

To make a request, contact the privacy email above. We will verify reasonable requests without collecting more information than necessary. An authorized agent may need to demonstrate authority. You may appeal a refusal under the process and deadlines required by applicable law.

07 / Age

Children and family accounts

Obscura does not knowingly collect children's personal data without the consent or other authorization required. If the app is designed for or marketed to children, the developer must separately review age rules, parental or guardian consent, advertising, SDKs, and app-store distribution requirements before release.

The age at which parental consent is required under the GDPR may vary by country. The US COPPA imposes specific obligations on services directed to children under 13 or that knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted data, contact us at the email above.

08 / Protection

Security and data incidents

We apply technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data and risks, such as limiting access, minimizing data, protecting transmissions, and reviewing providers. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

If an incident occurs, we will assess and contain it and meet applicable duties to notify users or authorities within the circumstances and time limits required by law.

09 / Updates

Policy changes

We will update the revision date on this page. If a change materially affects purposes, data categories, or your rights, we will provide notice by an appropriate method before or when the change takes effect, as required by law. Continued use of the app does not replace new consent where the law requires it.
